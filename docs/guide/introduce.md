# About BakaSU

## What is BakaSU? {#what-is-BakaSU}

A downstream fork of SukiSU Nova (origin: SukiSU Ultra),but updated to follow [`tiann/KernelSU`](https://github.com/tiann/KernelSU)

## Project Aim {#the-aim}

Keep everything simple and easy as possible, while maintaining newer and older kernel compatibility.
